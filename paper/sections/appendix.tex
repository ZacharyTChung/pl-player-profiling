\section{Minutes threshold sensitivity}
\label{sec:appendix:sensitivity}

The main analysis excludes outfield players below \MinMinutes{} minutes. That threshold
balances two errors: a low threshold admits players whose per-90 rates are dominated by
sampling noise, while a high threshold discards genuine squad roles such as the impact
substitute. Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} repeats the clustering at 270, \MinMinutes{} and
900 minutes and reports the eligible sample, the selected number of clusters, the
silhouette coefficient and the mean bootstrap adjusted Rand index at each.
